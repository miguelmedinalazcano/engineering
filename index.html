In short

Four seasons of Formula Student taught me to think about the whole car, not just one part. I led the design of the IFS-08 across chassis, suspension, aerodynamics, powertrain and HV/LV integration, and framed a two-year concept change that took 40 kg out of the car. For my BSc thesis I designed a CFRP monocoque and checked it against every rule load case. At Managing Composites I test structural carbon fibre parts, calibrate material cards, automate simulations, organise project folders and create tools that integrate AI.

Formula Student taught me how much work sits behind a design freeze, and how easily it can slip. Helping more than 80 people get there showed me the value of planning, communication and listening. I still have a lot to learn, and I would like to bring that experience to your team while learning from the engineers around me.
